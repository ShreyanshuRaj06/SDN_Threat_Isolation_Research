# Private Research Paper Manuscript (Overleaf / LaTeX)

This folder contains your private academic research paper draft, formatted in the official IEEE 2-column conference format (`IEEEtran`).

> **Privacy Notice:** This folder is added to `.gitignore` so that your unsubmitted paper text is NEVER indexed by public web crawlers or plagiarism scanners (Turnitin, iThenticate, IEEE CrossCheck).

---

### How to Open & Edit in Overleaf (2-Minute Setup):
1. Zip this `paper_manuscript` folder on your computer.
2. Go to [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com) and log in.
3. Click **New Project** > **Upload Project**.
4. Upload the zip file.
5. Overleaf will automatically compile `main.tex` into a dual-column IEEE conference paper PDF!

### Files in this Folder:
* `main.tex`: Full IEEEtran LaTeX manuscript including abstract, system architecture, mathematical rate formulas, empirical results table, and conclusions.
* `references.bib`: Complete BibTeX references (DistriNet CNS 2022, OpenFlow, XGBoost, Isolation Forest, SHAP, LSTM).
